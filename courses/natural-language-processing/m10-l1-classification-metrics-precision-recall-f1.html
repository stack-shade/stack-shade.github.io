What problem is Classification Metrics: Precision, Recall & F1 designed to solve?Reveal answer

What are the four stages of the mental model?Reveal answer

Why can a high benchmark score still fail in production?Reveal answer

A benchmark may not represent the deployment distribution, user goals, failure costs, or adversarial and edge cases encountered in practice.

Why is F1 not universally better than accuracy?Reveal answer

The useful metric depends on class imbalance and the relative costs of false positives versus false negatives.
