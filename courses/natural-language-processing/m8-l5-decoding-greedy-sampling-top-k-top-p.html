What is the key difference between causal and masked language modeling?Reveal answer

Causal models predict future tokens from past context under an autoregressive objective; masked models reconstruct hidden tokens using context on both sides.

Why use subword tokenization?Reveal answer

It balances vocabulary size with the ability to represent rare or unseen words by decomposing them into reusable pieces.
