ACTIVE RECALL
Why is tokenization a modeling decision rather than a harmless preprocessing step?

Because the token boundary determines the units the model sees. It affects vocabulary size, sequence length, rare-word handling and what information is easy or hard for the model to represent.

What can go wrong during aggressive normalization?

Useful distinctions such as case, punctuation, accents, formatting or domain-specific symbols can disappear, hurting downstream tasks.
