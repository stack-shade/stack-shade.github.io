ACTIVE RECALL
Why can't a process just access hardware directly?

Protection and fairness: one buggy/malicious process could corrupt everything. The CPU enforces privilege levels; only the kernel runs privileged instructions.

What does the timer interrupt guarantee?

The OS regains control periodically no matter what a process does — enabling preemptive multitasking.
