ACTIVE RECALL
Why do we learn DSA by patterns instead of by topics?

Because interview problems are designed to disguise a small set of underlying patterns. Recognizing the pattern triggers the template — topics don't.

What goes into a mistakes log?

The problem, the wrong approach you tried, WHY it failed, the trigger that should have pointed you to the right pattern, and the corrected template.
